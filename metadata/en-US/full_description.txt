FlareDo is an unofficial, free, and open-source client for the Linux.do forum.

Planned features include:

- Browse latest and popular topics as a guest
- Explore categories and tags
- Search topics and posts
- Read topic streams with safe rich-text rendering
- Sign in, create topics, reply, edit, and upload attachments
- Like and bookmark posts
- Read notifications and view member profiles
- Adaptive layouts for phones, tablets, foldables, and desktops

FlareDo targets Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, and Linux. Release builds will
contain no ads, tracking, telemetry, or third-party analytics.

This independent community project is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or
endorsed by Linux.do or Discourse.
